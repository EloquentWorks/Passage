# Changelog

All notable changes to Laravel Passage will be documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-25

### Added

- Fluent and configuration-defined passages
- Persistent enrollment, step progress, and audit models
- Required, optional, conditional, dependent, retryable, and expiring steps
- Progress snapshots and next-step resolution
- Automatic synchronization and completion conditions
- Middleware, events, reminders, commands, and model trait
- Configurable tables and models
- PHPUnit, PHPStan, Pint, CI, and full documentation
